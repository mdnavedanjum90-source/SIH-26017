## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, mission-critical command center aesthetic for geospatial intelligence and statutory land governance. Built for senior administrators, district collectors, and policy strategists overseeing compliance under the RFCTLARR Act 2013, the interface projects unquestioned authority, predictive clarity, and sovereign gravity.

The visual narrative synthesizes **Technical Brutalism** with **Cinematic Glassmorphism**:
- **Tactical Authority:** Monolithic structure, precise micro-borders, and rigorous tabular ergonomics convey institutional dependability and judicial exactitude.
- **Predictive Depth:** Luminous data overlays, dark-mode geospatial radiance, and optical depth evoke military-grade mission control displays.
- **Bilingual & Sovereign Dignity:** Seamless integration of regional administrative scripts alongside Latin technical copy, grounded by a subtle tri-color sovereign accent ribbon representing the Union of India.
- **Operational Cadence:** Immediate cognitive triage enabled by high-contrast telemetry rings, structured categorical tags, and strict visual prioritization of statutory bottlenecks.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for prolonged, low-fatigue monitoring under 24/7 operations room conditions. Deep void tones absorb ambient luminance, allowing critical data vectors and risk alerts to command optical priority.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#070B14`):** Deep ink background, providing absolute contrast for geospatial layers and vector charts.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#0F1626`):** Primary card and panel surface, providing depth separation from the canvas.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#162035`):** Interactive inputs, nested containers, hovered states, and toolbar trays.
- **Surface Layer 3 (`#1E2C47`):** Active selections, flyout overlays, and contextual modal backdrops.

### Sovereign Accent
- A hairline (2px) tri-color sovereign accent line is anchored strictly to the bottom edge of the persistent global header:
  - Deep Saffron: `#FF9933` (left segment / 33.33% width)
  - Pure White: `#FFFFFF` (center segment / 33.33% width)
  - India Green: `#138808` (right segment / 33.33% width)

### Core Accents
- **Primary Electric Teal (`#2DD4BF`):** Selected states, interactive focus rings, active GIS boundary vectors, and primary telemetry calls to action.
- **Secondary Atmospheric Blue (`#38BDF8`):** Informational links, secondary data dimensions, and cadastral parcel outlines.

### Statutory Risk Calibration (RFCTLARR Delay Modeling)
- **Low Risk (`#34D399` / Emerald):** On-schedule stages, undisputed surveys, environmental clearances granted.
- **Medium Risk (`#FBBF24` / Amber):** Section 11 preliminary notification lags, social impact assessment review delays.
- **High Risk (`#F97316` / Vermilion):** Section 19 declaration bottlenecks, compensation disbursement impasses.
- **Critical Risk (`#EF4444` / Crimson):** High Court litigation stays, Section 25 award lapse threshold (<60 days remaining), active civic disputes.

### Structural Lines & Borders
- **Subtle Partition:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` for all card perimeters, tabular rows, and modular splits.
- **Active Structural Highlight:** `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.40)` for focused or critical operational zones.

## Typography

The typography architecture uses a four-tier typographic hierarchy designed to separate command framing, procedural documentation, numerical readouts, and multilingual administrative records.

- **Primary Display (`Space Grotesk`):** Used for panel titles, module labels, and analytical section headers. Its geometric rigidity communicates technical command.
- **Operational Prose (`Inter`):** Deployed for statutory clauses, case remarks, field notes, and long-form administrative summaries requiring sustained legibility.
- **Command Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied exclusively to Khasra/survey plot numbers, RFCTLARR Act section references (e.g., `SEC-11(1)`, `SEC-19(1)`), delay duration countdowns, risk indices, and geographic coordinates. Tabular figures (`tnum`) must remain strictly enforced across all numeric views.
- **Regional Administrative Layer (`Noto Sans`):** Enforces Devanagari script parity for official gazette excerpts, tehsil/village names, and bilingual titles (e.g., भूमि अधिग्रहण स्थिति).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a high-density, mission-critical workspace model designed to optimize visual real estate across standard operations center monitors, multi-display command walls, and field-rugged laptops.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1440px and above):** 12-column fluid grid system with `gutter-lg` (24px) and outer `margin-lg` (32px). Supports persistent dual side-drawers (Left: Cadastral Hierarchy Tree; Right: Risk Vector Intelligence Feed).
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px):** 8-column layout with `gutter` (16px) and `margin-md` (24px). Analytical sidebars collapse into overlay slide-overs or stacked segmented tabs.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout with `gutter` (16px) and `margin` (16px). GIS viewport is fixed to the top viewport half with collapsible telemetry bottom-sheets.

### Vertical Density & Rhythm
- Strict 4px/8px baseline grid execution. Dense metadata groups use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).
- Analytical widgets, telemetry ring frames, and data modules adhere to an inner cell padding of `space-lg` (20px).
- Header bars, operational status strips, and tabular toolbars preserve a rigid vertical bounding height (e.g., 40px, 48px, or 64px) to ensure unified alignment across adjacent columns.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through luminous layering, calibrated frosted translucent planes, and dark-mode depth layering rather than traditional drop shadows.

### Glassmorphic Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid `#070B14`. The root foundation for static maps and spatial canvases.
- **Tier 1 (Structural Cards & Panes):** `#0F1626` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Enclosed by a uniform 1px solid border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Action Trays & Hover Cards):** `#162035` at 92% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Bordered by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, High-Priority Alerts & Dialogs):** `#1E2C47` at 98% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Outer border dynamically adopts the contextual risk color (e.g., `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.40)` for critical statutory delays).

### Luminous Depth & Glow
- Drop shadows are replaced with faint ambient light diffusion:
  - **Telemetry Focus Glow:** `0 0 20px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.15)` for active geospatial selections.
  - **Critical Alert Pulse:** `0 0 24px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.20)` for acquisition tracts at risk of statutory lapse.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, angular, and utilitarian geometry. Soft corners (Level 1: 4px default) maintain an architectural, instrument-like feel appropriate for judicial mapping and state land records.

- **Standard Elements (4px / 0.25rem):** Form inputs, buttons, table cell selections, micro-status tags, and card corners. This tight radius preserves structural grid alignment and high screen density.
- **Medium Panels & Modals (8px / 0.5rem):** Used exclusively for outer viewport dialogs, detached contextual menus, and floating map tool palettes.
- **Circular Indicators (100% / Full):** Reserved strictly for circular telemetry rings (0-100 risk gauges), micro-status LED pips, and parcel node anchors.
- **Prohibited:** Large pill shapes or bubbly curves, which compromise the authoritative operational tone.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Command:** Solid `#2DD4BF` with `#070B14` bold typography (`Space Grotesk` or `JetBrains Mono`). Hover state introduces an electric teal outer glow (`0 0 16px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.4)`). Active state scales slightly (98%).
- **Secondary Instrumental:** Background of `#162035` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` and `#F8FAFC` text. Hover shifts background to `#1E2C47` and border to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.24)`.
- **Destructive/Intervention:** Transparent background with 1px border of `#EF4444`, crimson text, transitioning to solid `#EF4444` on hover with crisp white text.

### 2. Status Chips & Badges
- Strict monospaced micro-badges (`JetBrains Mono`, 11px uppercase).
- Constructed using a 10% opacity tinted fill, 1px border at 30% opacity, and solid foreground text matched to the risk palette:
  - Low Risk: Tint `#34D399`
  - Medium Risk: Tint `#FBBF24`
  - High Risk: Tint `#F97316`
  - Critical Lapse Risk: Tint `#EF4444`

### 3. Circular Telemetry Gauge (0-100 Delay Index)
- SVG dual-ring radial component.
- Background track: 1px border thickness in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Active meter track: 3px stroke with round end-caps, color-mapped dynamically to the risk level.
- Center typography: Core index score in `telemetry-lg` (`JetBrains Mono`), with the sub-label (e.g., `DELAY SCORE`) in `label-caps`.

### 4. Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#0F1626`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`, text color `#F8FAFC`, placeholder color `#64748B`.
- Focus state triggers an immediate crisp 1px border of `#2DD4BF` and a subtle inner shadow `inset 0 0 4px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.20)`. No default browser outlines.

### 5. Cadastral Data Tables & Parcel Lists
- Alternating subtle rows (`transparent` vs `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`).
- Header row fixed in `#0B101D`, uppercase labels in `label-caps` (`#94A3B8`).
- Row borders: Hairline 1px bottom border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- Hover state: Row illuminates with `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.05)` and reveals quick-action GIS boundary locate buttons.

### 6. Specialized Component: Statutory Timeline Tracker (RFCTLARR Act 2013)
- Horizontal/vertical multi-stage progression node bar representing:
  1. `SIA` (Social Impact Assessment)
  2. `SEC-11` (Preliminary Notification)
  3. `SEC-19` (Declaration of Acquisition)
  4. `SEC-25` (Enquiry & Land Award)
- Each node features a status LED pip: solid teal (completed), flashing amber/red (stalled/lapse hazard), or muted gray (pending statutory window).
